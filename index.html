Audio Diagnostics

The game now plays all sounds via HTML5 audio. 🎰 Game sound is the real thing (a baked coin clink) — that's the one that matters. Web Audio tone is the OLD engine, kept only as a diagnostic; it may be silent on iOS, and that's now harmless.

Also check: the physical ring/silent switch on the side of the iPhone (orange = silent), the volume buttons while this page is open, and that audio isn't routed to AirPods/Bluetooth.

Game Mode

Off (default — booth/wristband mode): no coin step. Players just pull the lever for every go. A games-master stamps/removes wristband credits.
On (coin mode): players insert a £1 coin before each spin — the classic flow.
